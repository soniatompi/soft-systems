# Soft Systems  
### The Art of Human–AI Symbiosis

**Soft Systems** is an interactive digital magazine that explores the evolving relationship between humans and artificial intelligence through fashion, sound, text, and interface design.

Rather than presenting AI as a neutral tool or a dominant force, this project approaches it as a soft system: adaptive, intimate, and quietly influential. The magazine unfolds through a slow, tactile experience that invites reflection instead of instruction.

This repository contains the complete prototype.

---

## About the Project

Soft Systems examines how artificial intelligence and human emotion are already entangled in everyday life, often in subtle and unnoticed ways. Using fashion as both metaphor and interface, the magazine explores themes of control, agency, comfort, and ethical responsibility.

The experience is structured as a gradual shift in perspective:

1. **Poetic perception**  
   Emotion, embodiment, and metaphor  
2. **Informed understanding**  
   AI systems in fashion, personalization, and prediction  
3. **Personal positioning**  
   Reflection on ethics, responsibility, and conscious use  

The goal is not to warn or instruct, but to slow the conversation down.

---

## Experience

The magazine is accessed through a web-based 3D page-flipping interface.  
Users flip through the pages manually, creating a sense of agency and tactility similar to a physical publication.

Sound and visual pacing guide the experience quietly, without commands or prompts.

This slowness is intentional.

## Design Choices

- **Interface**  
  Page-flipping replaces scrolling to create tactility and slowness while remaining fully digital.

- **Visual Language**  
  All fashion visuals use a translucent, iridescent fabric to avoid aggressive futurism and instead suggest adaptability and softness.

- **Sound**  
  Ambient soundscapes and page-turn audio act as emotional anchors rather than instructions.

These choices reflect the project’s core belief: technology should adapt to human complexity, not flatten it.
