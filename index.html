ENCI | Photography & Material Archive

My artistic practice explores the interconnected relationships between nature, technology, human intervention, and the environments we inhabit. At ENCI, I began observing the landscape as a living archive where geological time, industrial history, and ecological transformation coexist. Through photography, material collection, scanning, printing, water marbling, three-dimensional painting, and sound, I investigated relationships between materials and systems. I invite you to explore the connections between these observations and experiments.
